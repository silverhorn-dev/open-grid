# Getting started

## 1. Open and install the app

Open your ${term.app} server's address in the browser. You can use ${term.app} right there – but it works best when you add it to your home screen:

- **Android (Chrome):** menu (⋮) → "Install app".
- **iPhone (Safari):** share icon → "Add to Home Screen".
- **Computer (Chrome, Edge):** "Install" icon in the address bar.

> On the iPhone, notifications only arrive when the app is installed on the home screen.

## 2. Choose a mode

On first start, ${term.app} asks how you want to use it:

- **Discover:** You see people nearby, and they see you.
- **Chat only:** Nobody sees you in an overview. Contacts only come through your invite link.
- **Friends nearby:** Only your contacts see you – and you see them.
- **Event:** Only people with the same event code, for 12 hours.

You can switch at any time in the menu (☰ top right) under **${ui:menu.mode}**.

## 3. Create your profile

Enter a name and fill in the fields you want to show. Add a profile picture and gallery later under **${ui:common.profile}**. Your profile is stored ${term.inBrowserStorage}; others get it directly from you.

## 4. The app at a glance

- **${ui:nav.grid}:** People near you.
- **${ui:common.chat}:** Your conversations.
- **${ui:common.profile}:** Profile, gallery and albums. The gear – or swiping left – takes you to the **${ui:common.settings}**.

## 5. Invite someone

At the top of the chat list there is **${ui:chat.invite}**. You get a link and a QR code. The link contains a security code: if anyone intercepted the connection, the app would notice.

## 6. Notifications

Under **${ui:common.settings} → ${ui:common.chat} → ${ui:settings.pushNotifications}**, tap **${ui:common.turnOnNotifications}**. By default the notification only says "New messages" – no name, no text.

## 7. Language

${term.app} is available in German and English: **${ui:common.settings} → ${ui:settings.general} → ${ui:settings.language}**.
