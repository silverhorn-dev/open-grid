# Welcome to OpenGrid

${text.appDescription} Discover people nearby and chat with them – without a company collecting your data.

**Your messages, photos and full profile stay on your device.** They travel ${term.p2p} to the person you are writing to and are ${term.encrypted}. ${text.serverSeesNothing}

## What you can do

- **Discover:** See who is nearby, send a flame and chat after a match.
- **Chat:** Messages, photos, snaps, voice messages and videos.
- **Friends:** Add contacts, share a status for 24 hours, see who is online.
- **Events:** With a shared event code you only see the people on site.

## What is different

- **No app store:** ${term.app} is ${term.aPwa}. Just add it to your home screen.
- **No account with email or phone number.**
- **No exact location:** The server only knows ${term.aCell}.
- **Free software:** Any community can ${term.selfHost}.
