# FAQ

### Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up with email or phone number. Your profile and keys are created on your device.

### What happens if I clear my browser data?

Then your profile, chats and keys are gone – they only live on your device. Install ${term.app} as an app; the browser then keeps the data permanently.

### My message doesn't arrive.

If the other person is offline, the message waits encrypted in the ${term.cache} and arrives once they are back online. Videos and the rest of long voice messages only travel over a ${term.directConnection} – so when you are both online at the same time.

### Why don't I get notifications?

Check **${ui:common.settings} → ${ui:common.chat} → ${ui:settings.pushNotifications}**. On the iPhone they only work when ${term.app} is installed on the home screen.

### Who can see my location?

Nobody exactly. Others only see a rough distance; the server only knows ${term.aCell}. With **${ui:settings.silentMode}** or **${ui:settings.safeZones}** you are completely invisible.

### Can someone screenshot my snaps?

Unfortunately yes – no app in a browser can prevent that. Instead, snaps and album images carry ${term.aWatermark}, which lets a shared image be traced.

### Can the operator read my chats?

No. Messages are ${term.encrypted}. The operator only sees that devices connect – not what you write.

### Is ${term.app} in the app store?

No, on purpose: ${term.app} is ${term.aPwa}. You install it straight from the browser.
